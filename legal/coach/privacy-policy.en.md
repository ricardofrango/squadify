# Squadify Coach — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}_

Squadify Coach ("the app") is published by **{{PUBLISHER_NAME}}** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what
data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and the rights you have over it.

If you have any question about this policy or your data, write to **{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}**.

## 1. Who the app is for

Squadify Coach is for adults who coach youth or adult teams: the coach creates an account and records their
teams, players, games and training sessions. Children don't use the app and can't create an account in it.

## 2. The data we collect

### 2.1 Your account

- **Your name and email address**, to create your account, sign you in and send you the account's emails
  (a password reset code, an email change code).
- **Your password**, stored only as a one-way hash: no one, us included, can read it.
- **Your phone number**, only if you choose to add it.
- **Your photo**, only if you choose to add one.

### 2.2 What you record about your teams

As you use the app you record data about your **age groups, teams, players, opponents, games and training
sessions**. About each player this can be: name, date of birth, shirt number, position, height, weight, an
emergency contact and a photo; and their call-ups, minutes, goals, cards and other events in games, ratings,
training attendance and the notes you write.

You decide what to record. Players are often children: **record a player's data only if you are allowed to**
(for example, with the consent of the player or of their parent or guardian, or because your club already
holds it for this purpose). For this data you decide what is recorded and why, and we keep and process it on
your behalf, only to provide the app to you and to the staff you invite.

### 2.3 Staff you invite

When you invite someone to join one of your age groups, we keep the invite and, if you send it by email, the
email address you sent it to.

### 2.4 Purchases

Paid plans are bought through **Google Play**. We receive from Google the details needed to give you the plan
you paid for (the plan, its period, its status and a purchase token). **We never see your card or payment
details**: Google handles the payment.

### 2.5 Notifications

If you allow notifications, your device gets a **push token** from Firebase Cloud Messaging, which we keep so
we can send you the app's notifications (a game scheduled or moved, a goal, the final score and so on). It's
removed when you sign out or delete your account.

### 2.6 Crash reports

When the app crashes, **Firebase Crashlytics** sends us a crash report: what went wrong in the code, the app's
version, the device model and Android version. It doesn't include your name, email or the data you recorded.
We use it only to fix the app.

### 2.7 Ads (Free plan only)

On the Free plan the app shows a banner ad on some screens (never during a live game) through **Google
AdMob**. To show ads, Google AdMob collects your device's **advertising ID**, your **approximate location**
(from your IP address) and how you interact with the ads.

- In the **European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland**, the app first asks for your consent
  (Google's consent form). Without your consent, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your
  choice at any time in the app's menu, **Privacy options**.
- Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
- Paid plans have no ads.

Google's use of this data is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

### 2.8 What we don't collect

We don't use any analytics or tracking tool of our own, we don't read your contacts, and we don't collect your
precise location.

## 3. Why we use your data (legal bases)

| What | Why | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| Account, teams, players, games, training | To provide the app you signed up for | Contract |
| Purchases | To give you the plan you paid for | Contract |
| Account emails | To keep your account secure (codes, changes) | Contract |
| Notifications | To tell you about your games | Consent (you allow notifications on your device) |
| Crash reports | To find and fix errors | Legitimate interest in a working app |
| Personalised ads (EEA, UK, Switzerland) | To fund the Free plan | Consent |
| Non-personalised ads | To fund the Free plan | Legitimate interest |

## 4. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We share it only with the services that run the app for us, and only for that:

- **Railway** (servers, database and photo storage).
- **Google Firebase** (Crashlytics crash reports, Cloud Messaging notifications).
- **Resend** (sending the account and invite emails).
- **Google Play** (purchases).
- **Google AdMob** (ads on the Free plan, as described in 2.7).

The **staff you invite** to an age group see that age group's players, games and training, as the app shows
them. We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

Some of these services are based in, or process data in, countries outside the European Economic Area, such
as the United States. Where they do, the transfer is covered by the safeguards the GDPR requires (such as the
European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Your account and everything you recorded** are kept while you have an account. When you **delete your
  account** (in the app: Account → Delete account) they're deleted straight away: your account, and if you own
  a team's space, the space and everything in it (age groups, teams, players, games, training, photos). If you
  were staff in someone else's space, you're removed from it; what you recorded there stays in that space,
  which belongs to its owner.
- **Sign-in sessions** expire after 30 days without use.
- **Codes sent by email** expire after a few minutes.
- **Crash reports** are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
- **Purchase records** may be kept for as long as the law requires for accounting.

## 6. Your rights

You can **see and correct** your account's details in the app (Account), and **delete your account and its
data** in the app at any time. You can also write to us at **{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}** to:

- get a copy of your data, or have it sent to another service;
- correct or delete it, or ask us to stop or limit using it;
- object to its use, or withdraw a consent you gave (for ads: Privacy options in the app's menu).

If you can't use the app any more, you can ask us to delete your account by writing to the same address from
your account's email. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in Portugal, the
**CNPD**, www.cnpd.pt).

## 7. Security

All data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. Photos are stored privately and
are only shown to people who can see that team. Only the staff of an age group see its data.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, if the change is significant, tell you in the
app.
